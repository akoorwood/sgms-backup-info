Privacy Policy

Effective: November 27, 2026

Data access and use

SGMS Backup uses authorized Google Drive access to create, locate, upload, download and verify backup files for business continuity and disaster recovery.

The configured Drive permission may allow broader file access, but the backup workflow is intended to operate within designated backup locations.

Data protection

Backup archives are encrypted before upload. Access credentials are restricted to authorized administrators and automated backup infrastructure.

Data sharing

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train artificial intelligence models. Infrastructure providers process data as needed to operate the authorized backup service.

Retention and deletion

Backup files are retained for disaster recovery. Authorized administrators may manage or delete backup files directly through Google Drive.

Revoking access

Google account access can be revoked through Google Account security settings. Revocation may interrupt automated backups.

Google API compliance

SGMS Backup's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Sainem Group administrator through the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
